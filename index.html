Our philosophy

The homes that show well are selling. The rest are leaving money behind.

In today's Toronto market, the gap between a designed home and an as-is listing isn't just aesthetic — it's measurable. Buyers pay a premium for homes that feel finished, intentional, and move-in ready.

Re House closes that gap before your listing goes live. We handle the design, the renovation, the staging, and the sale as one process — so you approve the plan, then re-engage at closing. Every renovation we recommend is chosen for one reason: return.
