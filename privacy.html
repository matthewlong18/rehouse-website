Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Re House (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through rehouse.ca, why we collect it, and how we handle it. We comply with Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us. That means:

  • Your name and email address, when you submit the enquiry form
  • Your property type and estimated sale timeline
  • Any additional details you share with us by email or on a call

We do not run advertising trackers on this site. Basic, anonymised traffic statistics may be collected by our hosting provider.

Why we collect it

Solely to respond to your enquiry, prepare a proposal for your property, and deliver our services. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone, ever.

Who can see it

Your information is accessible to the Re House team and to the service providers that operate our form and email tools on our behalf. Where a renovation requires it, we may share the minimum necessary details with a trusted contractor — with your knowledge.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are retained for as long as needed to serve you, and for a reasonable period afterwards for our records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your rights

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@rehouse.ca and we will respond within 30 days.

Cookies

This site does not set marketing or tracking cookies. If you submit the enquiry form, the form provider may set a functional cookie needed to process your submission.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@rehouse.ca.
